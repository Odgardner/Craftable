C R A F T A B L E

Guess the Minecraft crafting recipe

How to Play

Figure out a Minecraft crafting recipe in 10 attempts.

Controls

Click an item in the Inventory, then click a slot in the Crafting grid to place it — or the other way around, select a slot first. Click a filled slot again (or hit Clear Grid) to empty it. Once your arrangement matches any real recipe's shape, the output slot fills in and Craft & Submit lights up.

Feedback Colors

Right item, right spot in the shape
Right item, but the wrong spot
Not used — or you've already placed every copy the recipe needs

Matching works like real Minecraft: a shape counts as correct anywhere in the 3×3 grid, not just one fixed spot — the Crafting Table's 2×2 block, for example, is right in any of its four possible corners.

Remembering What You've Learned

Once an item scores green, yellow, or grey in a guess, its slot in the Inventory keeps that colour for the rest of the round — so you don't have to scroll back through past guesses to remember what you've already ruled out.

Daily vs. Random

In Daily mode, everyone gets the same recipe, which unlocks at midnight in your own timezone — same as Wordle. Switch to Random for unlimited practice: hit New Puzzle any time for a fresh, randomly picked recipe.
